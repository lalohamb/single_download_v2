waht is [Google Search Console] for?


Context
It's Google's free tool that shows you how your site performs in Google Search specifically.

What it tells you:

Which search queries people typed that led them to your site (e.g. "black graphic tees", "melanated t-shirt")

How many times your pages appeared in search results (impressions)

How many people actually clicked through (clicks)

Your average position in search results (rank)

Which pages are indexed by Google and which aren't

Crawl errors — pages Google couldn't read

Mobile usability issues

Core Web Vitals — how fast your pages load

Why it matters for Body & Sleeves:

You can see exactly what people are searching for when they find you — then optimize your product titles and descriptions around those terms

You can submit your sitemap so Google indexes all your products faster

If a product page isn't showing up in Google, you'll see why

You can request Google to re-index a page immediately after you update it

The difference from Google Analytics:

Search Console = what happens before someone clicks to your site (search behavior)

Google Analytics = what happens after someone lands on your site (on-site behavior)

Both are free and complement each other. Search Console is the one that directly impacts your SEO rankings.
