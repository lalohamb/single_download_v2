Your catalog stays in sync automatically. New product on Printify? It appears on your store. Price change? Reflected instantly. One-click sync from your admin dashboard — no manual work, ever. Supports all Printify blueprints, print providers, and shipping profiles.

### 📦 Zero-Waste Print-on-Demand
Every order is made fresh — no inventory, no overstock, no waste. Products ship directly from Printify's global fulfillment network to your customer. You never touch the product.

### 🔍 SEO Built In
Dynamic XML sitemap, robots.txt, per-product JSON-LD schema, Open Graph tags, canonical URLs, Twitter cards — all configurable from your admin panel. Get found on Google without hiring an SEO agency.

### 📱 Mobile-First Storefront
Responsive across all devices. Sticky header, slide-out cart drawer, collapsible mobile filters, touch-friendly product gallery with color swatches and size selectors. Customers can shop and checkout from their phone without friction.

### 📣 Email Marketing Included
Built-in MailerLite integration lets you manage your entire email list from your admin panel — add subscribers, create campaigns, build automations, track open rates and click rates. No separate tool to log into.

## FAQ

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. Everything is managed from your admin dashboard. No terminal, no config files, no developers.

**Does it work with my existing Printify account?**
Yes. You connect with your Printify API key. Your existing products, variants, and pricing sync instantly.

**What happens when I add a new product on Printify?**
Click "Sync from Printify" in your admin panel. New products appear immediately. You can then curate, feature, or categorize them.

**Do you handle fulfillment?**
We don't touch fulfillment — Printify does. When a customer pays, the order is automatically forwarded to Printify. They print and ship. You get paid.

**What payment processor do you use?**
Stripe. You connect your own Stripe account, so payments go directly to you. We never touch your money.

**Is there a transaction fee?**
No. We charge a flat monthly subscription. Stripe charges their standard processing fee (2.9% + 30¢), which is between you and Stripe.

**Can I use my own domain?**
Yes. All plans include custom domain support.

**What's included in the free trial?**
Full access to all features on your chosen plan for 14 days. No credit card required to start.

## Key Conversion Principles

1. **Lead with the free trial** — "Free to start" removes the biggest objection immediately.
2. **Specificity sells** — "5 minutes" beats "fast". "14-day trial" beats "try it free".
3. **Anchor on the Printify seller's pain** — They have products but no real store. Every section should name that pain, then resolve it.
4. **Show the admin dashboard** — Screenshots or a live demo of the dashboard is the single highest-converting asset. It makes the product real.
5. **No transaction fees** — This is a major differentiator vs. Shopify. Say it clearly.
6. **Printify does the fulfillment** — Reinforce that they never touch inventory. Zero-risk business model.
